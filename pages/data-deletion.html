User Data Deletion Instructions

Target Platforms: WhatsApp Business API & Web Standard SLA: Immediate to 48 Hours
🗑️ Your Right to Erasure

In accordance with Meta Platform Terms (Section 4.f) and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act, India), AskKisan provides simple, transparent mechanisms for any user or farmer to request complete deletion of their account records, phone number association, and query history.

01. Method 1: Instant In-Chat Deletion (WhatsApp)

If you interact with AskKisan over WhatsApp, you can trigger automated data deletion immediately inside your chat thread without needing to send an email:

1

Open WhatsApp Chat

Launch WhatsApp and open your active conversation thread with the AskKisan assistant.

2

Send Deletion Keyword

Type and send one of the following exact command phrases:

DELETE MY DATA  or  PURGE ACCOUNT
3

Receive Instant Confirmation

The AskKisan system will immediately process your request, purge your stored chat memory and session index, and reply with a confirmation message verifying your records have been erased.

02. Method 2: Email Request to Privacy Desk

Alternatively, if you cannot access WhatsApp or prefer a formal verification ticket, you may submit a manual data deletion request to our Privacy Desk:

✉️

Send an Email Request

Recipient: ask.kisan@gmail.com

Subject: Data Deletion Request - [Your Phone Number]

Body: Please specify your WhatsApp phone number with country code (e.g., +91 98765 43210) and request the permanent erasure of all associated interaction records.

Timeline: Our privacy officer will verify identity via one-time SMS confirmation and purge all associated records within 48 hours. You will receive an official confirmation receipt.

03. What Data is Permanently Deleted?

Executing a deletion request removes all personal and interaction data from our operational databases:

  • Phone Number Identifier: The primary key mapping your WhatsApp MSISDN to AskKisan user tables.
  • Chat History & Transcripts: All past inbound and outbound question-and-answer pairs stored for conversational context.
  • Farmer Preferences: Any voluntarily provided district, state, preferred language, or crop context parameters.
  • Generated Context Embeddings: Any vectorized representation of your user-specific query history.

04. Retention Exceptions

In strict compliance with statutory regulations, basic anonymized transaction audit logs (with phone numbers and identifying details irreversibly hashed or stripped) may be retained solely where mandated by law for cyber security incident reporting or anti-abuse protection.

05. Meta Platform Compliance Note

This page serves as the official Data Deletion Instructions URL required by Meta App Review under the WhatsApp Business Cloud API policy. If Meta platform users initiate data deletion through Meta’s User Settings, our callback endpoint and manual desk fulfill requests under the same 48-hour SLA.
