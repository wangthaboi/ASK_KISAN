Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 Version: 1.2 (DPDP Act & WhatsApp Cloud API Compliant)
🛡️ Our Commitment to Indian Farmers

AskKisan is built to provide trustworthy agricultural knowledge to farmers across India. We believe farmer data privacy is sacred. We minimize data collection, never sell personal information, and respect your right to access and delete your records at any time.

01. Introduction & Scope

AskKisan ("we", "our", or "the Platform") operates an AI-assisted agricultural advisory platform accessible via messaging services (including the WhatsApp Business Cloud API) and web interfaces. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, process, and protect your personal data in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act, India), Information Technology rules, and Meta Platform Policies.

02. Information We Collect

We adhere strictly to the principle of data minimization. We only collect the minimal information necessary to deliver agricultural answers:

  • Phone Number (WhatsApp MSISDN): When contacting us via WhatsApp, your phone number serves as your unique account and session identifier to enforce daily usage limits and maintain context.
  • Agricultural Query Information: Crop names, pest or disease symptoms, weather or soil conditions, and general district/state location that you voluntarily share to obtain localized advisories.
  • Chat & Interaction Logs: Recent conversation history used strictly to provide relevant multi-turn follow-up answers during your active session.
⚠️ Information We NEVER Solicit or Store

AskKisan will never ask for, nor should you ever share: Aadhaar numbers, PAN details, bank account numbers, UPI PINs, passwords, or credit/debit card credentials. The platform will never initiate requests for financial transactions.

03. Purpose of Data Processing

Your information is processed strictly for the following legitimate agricultural purposes:

  1. AI Advisory Generation: Grounding queries in verified agronomic reference materials, government schemes, and crop protection datasets.
  2. Session Continuity: Enabling multi-turn advisory conversations (e.g., following up on a pest treatment recommendation).
  3. Service Stability & Quota Management: Enforcing fair-usage tiers and preventing automated denial-of-service spam.
  4. Continuous Quality Improvement: Evaluating anonymized agronomic query trends to expand relevant agricultural knowledge bases.

04. Third-Party Processors & Infrastructure

To provide reliable service, we interface with trusted cloud and infrastructure providers who maintain high security standards:

  • Meta Cloud API (WhatsApp): Message transmission and delivery under Meta’s WhatsApp Business Terms.
  • Database Hosting (Supabase PostgreSQL): Encrypted storage for user tiering and session persistence with Row Level Security.
  • AI Inference Services (Google Gemini & OpenAI): Query synthesis and language parsing. Data transmitted for model inference is strictly governed by zero-data-retention and enterprise confidentiality policies.

05. Data Retention & Security Measures

We implement administrative and technical controls to safeguard your data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. Inactive conversation history is routinely pruned. We do not retain farmer queries longer than necessary to support active sessions, troubleshoot platform bugs, or comply with applicable legal mandates.

06. Your Rights under the DPDP Act, 2023

As a Data Principal under Indian law, you possess explicit rights regarding your digital personal data:

  • Right to Access: Request a summary of personal data held about you and processing activities.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: Request deletion or correction of outdated or inaccurate personal records.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: Direct complaints to our designated Grievance Officer.
  • Right to Nominate: Nominate an individual to exercise your data rights in the event of death or incapacity.

To exercise your Right to Erasure, refer to our simple step-by-step User Data Deletion Instructions.
